# India and the World, Simply Briefed: episode plan

40 episodes × 5 minutes ≈ 3.3 hours, in 6 units, plus a vertical Short for each episode and, from December 2026, a monthly "World This Month" episode. ★ = heavily searched and likely in the exam; make these first.

**Web thread:** the labelled thread each episode adds to the relations web (facts only, no judgements). **Refresh by:** when `/refresh` should re-check the episode. Topics and angles are a starting point: re-check each against the news when scripting.

Status: **to script** · **sample script (approve)** · **scripted** = approved · **done** = video and Short finished · **refresh (approve)** = changed lines waiting for approval · **needs attention**.
