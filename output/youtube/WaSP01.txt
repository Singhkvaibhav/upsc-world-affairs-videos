TITLE
Nvidia, Jensen Huang and the Honey-Trap Question | GPUs, HBM Memory and the AI Buildout | UPSC Special

DESCRIPTION
As of 9 November 2026. On 8 November 2026, Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation into its founder, Jensen Huang, after reports that he was the target of a Chinese intelligence effort. This special explains who Jensen Huang is (as he told it on The Joe Rogan Experience #2422), what a GPU is and why AI needs one, why memory makers like SK Hynix, Samsung and Micron matter, what the allegations are and what each side says, what it could mean for the AI buildout, and why it matters for India.

The allegations are reported, not proven, and no one has been charged. Every claim is attributed to the side that makes it.

Memory tip: MICE (Money, Ideology, Compromise, Ego). A honey trap works through compromise.
